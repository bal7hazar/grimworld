## 2026-09-28

- **SPK-2 cost spike** ([#25](https://github.com/bal7hazar/grimworld/pull/25)): the same worst cases on Dojo 1.8 and as native contracts, on one devnet and one Cairo 2.19 account. Native worst tick under D-127: 5.16M L2 gas (0.27× Dojo). A 300-action expedition: $0.69 to $0.93 natively ($1.21 to $2.83 on Dojo), against ADR-0001's $0.50: **the threshold does not hold at today's prices**. Research: [docs/research/SPK-2-cost.md](docs/research/SPK-2-cost.md).
- **FND-02 continuous integration** ([#21](https://github.com/bal7hazar/grimworld/pull/21)): workflow `ci` on every pull request and push to `main`: one job per Cairo package or workspace on the toolchain of its nearest `.tool-versions` (validated before any download; snforge and its compiler from pinned release archives), and a client job; read-only, no secret, every action pinned by SHA. Report: [docs/reports/FND-02-ci.md](docs/reports/FND-02-ci.md).
- **FND-01b native scaffold** ([#27](https://github.com/bal7hazar/grimworld/pull/27)): `contracts/` is a Cairo 2.19 workspace of `grimworld_logic` (pure, with `origami_hexmap` 1.8.0, which now builds), `grimworld_persistent` and `grimworld_ephemeral` (contracts `Persistent`, `Ephemeral`, deployed by the tests); `client/` on starknet.js 10.8.0, no Dojo. Report: [docs/reports/FND-01b-scaffold-native.md](docs/reports/FND-01b-scaffold-native.md).
- **SPK-5b toolchain without Dojo** ([#24](https://github.com/bal7hazar/grimworld/pull/24)): Cairo 2.19 (Scarb 2.19.4, snforge and sncast 0.61.0), **starknet-devnet 0.10.0** as the local node (Katana refuses Cairo 2.19 classes), starknet.js 10.8.0; `scripts/with-node.sh` replaces `scripts/with-katana.sh`; `scripts/lock.sh` accepts `scarb --manifest-path <path> <subcommand>`; the Dojo spikes keep their own pins and wrappers. Report: [docs/reports/SPK-5b-toolchain-native.md](docs/reports/SPK-5b-toolchain-native.md).
- **Profile for native Starknet and Rust spikes** ([#20](https://github.com/bal7hazar/grimworld/pull/20)): `implement` allows Rust builds confined to the worktree with the installed toolchains, `sncast` against a local node only, and `starknet-devnet`. Audit: [docs/reports/PR-20-profile-audit-gpt-6-sol.md](docs/reports/PR-20-profile-audit-gpt-6-sol.md).
- **ART-00 asset pipeline** ([#12](https://github.com/bal7hazar/grimworld/pull/12)): `tools/art/build.py` cleans, renames and packs the MVP sprites (5 castes, 3 professions) from the private `assets` submodule into PixiJS 8 atlases in the ignored `tools/art/out/`; `CREDITS.md`. Report: [docs/reports/ART-00-asset-pipeline.md](docs/reports/ART-00-asset-pipeline.md).
- **FND-01 repository scaffold** ([#18](https://github.com/bal7hazar/grimworld/pull/18)): `contracts/` (Dojo package `grimworld`, namespaces `grimworld` and `grimworld_instance`, layering of CONTEXT §4) and the pnpm workspace `client/` (`@grimworld/sim` without rendering or chain dependency, `@grimworld/app` with PixiJS on demand). `origami_hexmap` 1.8.0 does not build on Cairo 2.13 (N-9). Report: [docs/reports/FND-01-scaffold.md](docs/reports/FND-01-scaffold.md).
- **SPK-5 toolchain pins** ([#14](https://github.com/bal7hazar/grimworld/pull/14)): the game is on **Cairo 2.13** (Scarb 2.13.1, snforge 0.51.2), Dojo 1.8 (sozo 1.8.7, Katana 1.7.1, Torii 1.8.16, `dojo` 1.8.0), Node 24.21.0, pnpm 12.5.1, dojo.js 2.0.0; `scripts/setup-toolchain.sh`, `scripts/with-katana.sh`; Torii self-hosted (Slot retired). Report: [docs/reports/SPK-5-toolchain.md](docs/reports/SPK-5-toolchain.md).
- **Launcher thresholds** ([#11](https://github.com/bal7hazar/grimworld/pull/11)): no agent starts or resumes above a 5-minute load of 12 or under 8 GB available (`scripts/agent.sh thresholds`); codex started through the system `node`; the `implement` profile denies direct agent-launch commands.
