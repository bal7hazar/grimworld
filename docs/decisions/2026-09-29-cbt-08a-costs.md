## 1. `set_build` costs about 3.2 times ENG-01's estimate

4.41 M net (E) against §10's 1,369,635. The bulk is registry reads: every bar skill's `SKILL` record and
every equipped item's `BASE` record (its slot and hands), 7 `BASE` reads alone being 14 slots.
`set_build` is not on the expedition's path; a player pays it when changing the build in a hub.

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| (a) **Copy the base's slot and hands into `ItemBase`'s free bits 120–127 when an item is created** (that word is written anyway; CAIRO §1 prefers a stored field) | removes the 7 `BASE` reads and most of the equipment's share; no interface change; the item's creator (loot, craft) writes two more fields |
| (b) A registry view of part 0 only (a `headers` read) for `SKILL` and `BASE` | saves about 15 slot reads; a change to the registry's interface |
| (c) Accept 4.41 M as the target | none |

**Recommendation: (a)** in CBT-08a's fix loop, then the measured figure as the target under
D-144's rule.

## Decision

**D-158**, `[Opus 5.5]` project manager, 2026-09-29, under D-128.

1. **`set_build`: option (a)**. The slot and the hands of an item are copied into `ItemBase`'s free
   bits when the item is created, in CBT-08a's fix loop; the measured figure after it becomes the
   target. `set_build` is a hub action, not on the expedition's path; if it still passes the hub
   budget of the cost budget (2.0M) after (a), it comes back to the project manager.
2. **The belt's worst case: targets accepted**, `enter` **5,250,000**, `leave` to a hub
   **3,100,000**, `travel_back` **2,900,000**; `enter` without a belt at its measure (+10.1 %, CBT-01's
   larger snapshot). About +$0.002 on S1. Question F (the snapshot packed, D-157) stays the lever,
   decided with ENG-07.
3. **The expedition's drift is tracked**: D-148 and this decision add about +$0.003 to S1's
   $0.556 of ENG-01 §10.2. ENG-07's measure of a tick inside a batch decides R-2; the orchestrator
   writes S1's running estimate in STATUS at each lot that moves it.

**What would reverse it**: ENG-07 finding S1 above $0.50 with the tick at its best, in which case
the snapshot (F) and the belt's reads are the first levers pulled.
