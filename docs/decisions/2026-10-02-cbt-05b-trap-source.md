# D-188: CBT-05b Q6: the source and payload of a terrain trap

| | |
|---|---|
| Decided by | project manager, 2026-10-02; recorded by the project manager |

## Decided

A terrain trap's source is its location band's lower level, rank 0. Its payload is the skill's entries without its TRAP entry.

## Why

The brief left the source open; the lower level is the weakest reading, safe to strengthen.

## What would reverse it

Playtest showing traps too weak in the band's upper half, or the owner's design reading.
