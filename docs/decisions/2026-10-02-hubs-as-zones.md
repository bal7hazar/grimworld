# D-202: hubs are lived like exploration zones on the client

| | |
|---|---|
| Decided by | owner, 2026-10-02, relayed by the Overseer; recorded by the project manager |

## Decided

On the client a hub is lived like an exploration zone: the same hex map, camera, pathfinding and rendering. Its places are buildings the adventurer walks to. Nothing changes on chain (D-03 stands). CLI-03f implements it ([#319](https://github.com/bal7hazar/grimworld/pull/319), open; its brief [#317](https://github.com/bal7hazar/grimworld/pull/317), open). CLI-03e's illustrated hub is superseded.

## Why

The owner's words: "whether town, exploration zone or dungeon, we must model on the exploration-zone experience (hex map, pathfinding, etc.)".

## What would reverse it

The owner's word.
