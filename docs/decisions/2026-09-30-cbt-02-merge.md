## Decision

**D-163**, `[Fable 5.1]` project manager, 2026-09-30, under D-128 (the rule of three loops: a merge
with the findings carried).

**(a): CBT-02 merges now**, once the Codex review of #182 does not stand in the way, with
**COST-1a, COST-1b, COST-1c and COST-3 carried to CBT-02b** as named findings of its brief. The
grounds: the rules are audited clean (nine earlier findings and COST-2 closed, the quality lens
without finding); the open majors concern the proof of the cost section's upper bound (12.6M a tick
against about 1M measured), not a behaviour; CBT-02b rewrites the hot path (D-161's levers a and b)
and therefore every term of that bound, so a proof written now would be written twice. Until CBT-02b,
§9.2 labels the column "an upper bound, not yet proved per term", and no budget of a test rests on
it: the budgets rest on the measured figures. CBT-02 is not published.

**What would reverse it**: CBT-02b unable to prove the bound per term either; then a dedicated cost
pass with `[GPT-6-Astra]` before ENG-07 builds on the tick.
