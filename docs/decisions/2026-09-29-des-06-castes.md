## Decision

**D-160**, `[Opus 5.5]` project manager, 2026-09-29, under D-128.

1. **The 33 questions are decided as the auditor's view states them** (the archived audit, one line
   each); the questions for after the MVP (DS-14, 16, 25 to 28) are recorded, not built.
2. **DS-9, the strength cap: design/04's rule as written**, weapon strength = 5 × the attribute rank,
   **capped by level**; BAL-01 tunes the curve. "No cap" is refused: the proved bounds of D-157 G
   rest on a cap.
3. **One last pass of DES-06, narrow**, as for DES-04 (D-155): the decisions written into design/20,
   each marked decided, nothing else; a short `[GPT-6-Astra]` check; the merge. CBT-02 takes DS-1 to
   DS-9 from this decision; if it lands first, its hook for D-157 G stays and a follow-up applies
   the bounds. **The restrictions the capacity proof relies on (validators, flattening checks,
   acceptance tests) are built before any production snapshot.**

**What would reverse it**: the owner's reading of a caste; BAL-01 finding a cap that breaks a
profession's identity.
