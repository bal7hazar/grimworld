## Decision

**D-155**, `[Opus 5.5]` project manager, 2026-09-29, under D-128 (the rule of three loops applied:
a fourth loop limited to named findings).

1. **The 43 recommendations are accepted as written**; **FX-26** (the Seal of Capture) is deferred
   to after the MVP, as the auditor says. Each is marked decided in `docs/design/19-effects.md`.
2. **F-20: signed aggregation of armor, with D-140's zero floor** (armor after bonuses and
   penetration never below 0); the guarded aggregate's encoding and bounds written. Negative armor
   stays permitted in the catalogue: forbidding it would remove debuffs the design names, for no
   saving. **F-16 is ENG-05's**: the three-call figure is an estimate until ENG-05's reveal defines
   the read schedule (D-145's measurement). **F-15**: written in both executor branches.
3. **One last pass, narrow**: F-20, F-15 and the decisions of 1 written, nothing else; a short
   `[GPT-6-Astra]` check of that pass; then the merge. A new finding outside these is recorded for
   CBT-01, not fixed in DES-04, unless it is a blocker.

**What would reverse it**: the owner's reading of a combat rule (the report's §9 lists them; the
owner may reverse any one, which moves the vectors of CBT's lots, not a frozen interface yet).
