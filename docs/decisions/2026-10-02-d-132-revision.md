## The owner's words

> Délégation, révision de D-132 : pour quiver_quest et quiver_achievement, le chef de projet Grim World donne lui-même le go de publication des versions stables quand la checklist D-132 passe, l'inscrit comme décision, et me prévient après coup. In English: *Delegation, revision of D-132: for quiver_quest and quiver_achievement, the Grim World project manager gives the go for publishing stable versions itself when the D-132 checklist passes, records it as a decision, and tells me afterwards. The other packages stay at my go. Record it on main.*

## Decided

D-132 is revised for `quiver_quest` and `quiver_achievement` (stable versions): the project manager gives the go itself when the D-132 checklist passes, records it as a decision, and tells the owner afterwards. Every other package stays at the owner's go. Release candidates stay the project manager's (D-132 as narrowed).

## Why

The checklists of the 0.2.0 versions of both packages passed on 2026-10-02; the owner's go was the only thing left to wait for.

## What would reverse it

The owner's word. Extending it to another package needs the owner's go.

## Sources

D-132; the owner's delegation quoted above (2026-10-02).
