## 3. What the batch finding asks

A worst tick fills a transaction by itself even after every lever. Either the worst tick must be made
impossible by rule (fewer awake goblins, fewer targets, a cap on what one tick resolves), or a batch must
stop before a heavy tick (ENG-07's batch weight already measures each tick), or the per-tick budget of
ADR-0001 (L2 only, D-129's $0.50 for S1) is reopened with SPK-12 (client-side proving). **For the
owner**, with the project manager's recommendation.

**Recommendation (the orchestrator's)**: (a) ENG-07's batch weight stops a batch before a tick that would
pass the transaction's limit (the worst tick then runs alone, at its cost), so no rule changes for the
worst case; (b) ENG-07 defines and measures a representative fight tick first, and S1 is judged on it;
(c) the design levers are weighed by the owner on (b)'s figure, not on the worst tick's.

## Decision

**D-172**, `[Fable 5.1]` project manager, 2026-10-01, under D-128, on §1 and on what is the project
manager's in §3; §2 and the rest of §3 are put to the owner with a recommendation.

1. **The engineering levers**: **L3** (the executor) into CBT-05's brief, **L4** (one-pass perception)
   into ENG-07's, **L2** (applications in place) into CBT-04's fix loop after Codex's audit, or CBT-05
   if that loop is closed first; none touches a frozen interface. **L1** is ENG-07's, on its own count
   of the hooks' writes to frozen goblins, since it changes `load`'s contract with perception.
2. **The batch**: ENG-07's batch weight **stops a batch before a tick that would pass the
   transaction's limit**, so that the worst tick never breaks a batch, only shortens it; **no rule
   changes for the worst case now**. ENG-07 **defines and measures a representative fight tick
   first** (a hit, an application, goblins awake as a typical room has them), and S1 and the design
   levers are judged on that figure, not on the worst tick.
3. **Put to the owner** (PROGRAMME, *Waiting for the owner*), with the project manager's
   recommendation:
   - **The design levers (§2)**: recommended **not now**: D1 (fewer awake goblins) and D2′ (a
     bomb's targets) change the feel of a fight for a worst case that the batch weight already
     contains, and they move the representative tick by 0.13–0.26M only; to be judged on ENG-07's
     representative tick, with the fun gate (M4) in view. D3 (a smaller window) is refused by
     D-120's reasoning; D4 (20 ticks a batch) is ENG-07's measure, not a rule.
   - **The per-tick budget**: no measured combination reaches 1.47M a tick (the best is about 11.8M
     with every lever, E). The threshold of $0.50 for 300 actions on L2 alone (D-129, ADR-0001)
     is therefore **not reachable for a fight-heavy expedition**; it may hold for a light one. The
     project manager recommends **keeping the threshold as the target, judging it on the
     representative tick, and treating SPK-12 (client-side proving) as the structural answer**:
     the owner decides whether ADR-0001 is reopened on SPK-12's report, and whether the threshold
     is restated with the business model (Q-10).

**What would reverse it**: ENG-07's representative fight tick near 1.47M (then the threshold holds
and SPK-12 stays a study for version 1); or far above it (then the design levers come to the owner
with numbers, before CBT-06).
