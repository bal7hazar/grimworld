## Decision

**D-168**, `[Fable 5.1]` project manager, 2026-09-30, under D-128: **(a), as CBT-02e**, with three
conditions.

1. **The flattening runs once, at `set_build`, in a library class**, and its result, the snapshot,
   is **stored with the adventurer as packed words** (D-157 F is thereby settled early: packed, not
   63 felts); `enter` copies it. The two frozen changes are decided: `set_contracts` gains the
   flattening class's hash (ENG-01 §1.3's rule for pure rules in library classes), `Hub`'s storage
   gains the snapshot words. `set_build`'s target is its measure, a hub action (D-158 (c)); `enter`
   returns under D-158's 5.25M or comes back to the project manager with the figure.
2. **A snapshot is never stale on the expedition's path.** Every entrypoint that changes an input of
   the flattening (the build, an equipment slot, a rune, the belt, a level, a skill learnt) either
   recomputes the snapshot or marks it stale, and `enter` **refuses a stale snapshot** with a named
   error (the client sends `set_build` first). CBT-02e lists those entrypoints, present and planned
   (GLD-01's level-up, RWD-06's equipment), and tests the refusal. ADR-0001's rule stands: the
   ephemeral domain reads a snapshot taken at entry, here the stored one.
3. **Cost of the storage**: the snapshot's words are new slots at an adventurer's first `set_build`
   (about 453,524 each, once per adventurer), overwrites after; CBT-02e states the count of words
   and the figure, and the cost budget's hub row takes it.

CBT-02c merges unwired once audited (its registry checks and linear flattening stand); **no
production snapshot before CBT-02e** (D-160); nothing deployed.

**What would reverse it**: `Hub` still above 50 % with the flattening out of it (then the snapshot's
storage moves to `Instances`'s persistent side or a third contract, a decision of the project
manager); the stale-snapshot rule proving unplayable at the fun gate (then `enter` recomputes, at
its cost).
