# D-205: who decides

| | |
|---|---|
| Decided by | owner, 2026-10-03, in the organisation's standard; recorded by the project manager |

## Decided

The owner decides the reserved list and what they set themselves (the vision or scope of a programme, dropping a track, a licence). Every other reversible choice inside the repositories is the project manager's for the programme (an orchestrator's for its track alone), recorded with what would reverse it. "For the owner's eye" means: decide, merge, show; the owner's remark comes later as a change to make, never as a go to wait for.

## What would reverse it

The owner's word.
