## 1. OP-1: what an unrevealed chunk is, in a dungeon as in a zone (D-136)

| Rule | |
|---|---|
| **In the window, a chunk that is not revealed is wall** | In zones and in dungeons alike. The assembly takes a constant for it, as for a void chunk. Nothing is simulated in it: its goblins do not exist yet |
| A chunk is revealed by the move that brings **sight** onto it | The chain generates it in that transaction; the client generates the same chunk from the entry draw and the adventurer's irreversible actions (D-111), and reconciliation checks it |
| Sight never reaches a chunk that is not revealed | Sight has radius 6 and the ring of the window is 7 tiles away: what the window overlaps beyond sight is its ring and the corners of its rectangle, which are wall for the computation anyway |
| In a dungeon, whether a chunk exists beyond an edge | Is decided when the chunk that owns the edge is revealed: an open edge has a neighbour that will be generated when sight reaches it; a border edge has none, and what lies beyond is void (D-134). The client predicts it with the chunk |
| So the client never waits for a chunk | It reads what is revealed, generates what its move reveals, and takes wall for the rest |

This closes finding F-14 of DES-21's audit for dungeons. ENG-01 takes the rule as it is;
ENG-05 and the map library's N-3 assemble an unrevealed chunk as they assemble a void one.

## 2. The amendments DES-21 asks

**Accepted as written in DES-21's report**, Escalations, items 1 to 6. They are consequences
of D-133, which the project manager decided. The game orchestrator writes them in one pull
request of documents and merges it on completed green checks.

| Document | Amendment |
|---|---|
| CONTEXT §5 | Glossary: queue (planned), batch, played and planned, rewind, sequence, weight, copy of the instance, recovery |
| ADR-0001 | Played actions travel in batches; the client rewinds to any depth; rate limits are counted in gas; the client installs chain state only from one snapshot or its pinned copy |
| ADR-0002 | A Fate action ends a batch and a planned queue; the multicall rules; every precondition checked before a draw; what version 1 requires of a provider (a draw that does not change between attempts, no re-roll by a conditional abort) |
| ADR-0006 | The two lines that describe option A of the random word: under option C a reveal is computed and rides in a batch; the stop at a reveal is a rule of planned queues, evaluated by the client |
| ADR-0007 | No indexer for the state of the simulation |
| D-05 | Actions played and not yet sent are lost when the adventurer is resumed on another device |
| design/07, 09, 18 | "Action queue" becomes "planned queue" or "batch" |

The bound of 40M L2 gas on a batch is a target computed from SPK-7 and SPK-1b: it is
recomputed when either figure moves, and ENG-01 proves it before the weights are frozen.
