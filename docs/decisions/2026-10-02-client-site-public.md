# D-201: the client of main is published, public, with the tiny-swords atlas only

| | |
|---|---|
| Decided by | owner, 2026-10-02, relayed by the Overseer; recorded by the project manager |

## Decided

The client of `main` is published at grimworld.bal7hazar.com, public (no protection), and updated from `main` by a user timer ([#316](https://github.com/bal7hazar/grimworld/pull/316), merged 5b72373). It serves the tiny-swords pack's built atlas and `sprites.json` only: never the pack's raw files, and nothing of the pack in a public repository. D-73 is otherwise unchanged.

## Why

The owner's words: "no need for protection on the grimworld client", and "the assets are weird old birds, we are supposed to use the tiny-swords assets".

## What would reverse it

The owner's word.
