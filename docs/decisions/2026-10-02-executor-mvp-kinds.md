# D-197: the executor covers the MVP's content only

| | |
|---|---|
| Decided by | project manager, 2026-10-02; recorded by the project manager |

## Decided

CBT-05a's executor covers only the effect kinds and shapes the MVP's content uses. LIFE_STEAL, INTERRUPT and the entry guards ABOVE_HALF, IN_STANCE and ENCHANTED are refused by the validators (tested) and deferred to a PLAN row "executor kinds beyond the MVP's content" (first sources: the Gravecaller, the Champion's Disrupting Chop). A scope deferral, not a design change: they stay in design/19.

## Why

The executor did not fit its class (D-198); the deferred kinds are not used by the MVP's content.

## What would reverse it

MVP content that needs one of them, or the owner.
