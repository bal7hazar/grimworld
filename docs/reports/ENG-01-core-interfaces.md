# [Opus 5.5] ENG-01 — Freeze the core interfaces, under a cost budget

## Summary

Pull request: https://github.com/bal7hazar/grimworld/pull/81. CI is green (every job passes, including `cairo (contracts)`). Not merged.

The model in this session's system prompt is Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`), which is the model the brief names.

What exists now that did not before:

- **Five contracts, frozen as code that compiles.** `Instances` (ephemeral domain), plus `Hub`, `Market`, `Registry` and `TxHashFate` (persistent domain). For each:
  - the interfaces, the storage structs with explicit bit layouts, and the events;
  - every game entrypoint is a stub that reverts with `'not implemented'`;
  - constructors and `version` are implemented;
  - `TxHashFate` is implemented: `poseidon(tx hash, domain)`, and it refuses mainnet both at deployment and at every call (tested).
- **Additions to the shared package `grimworld_logic`:**
  - identifiers and bounds, and the enums `Stop`, `Refusal`, `Outcome`, `ChunkKind`, `Target`;
  - the wire format of a played batch: 1 to 10 actions in **one felt** of calldata;
  - the snapshot taken at entry and the task pages (D-131);
  - the packing rules: two limbs, and **`LIVE` at bit 250 in every stored record**;
  - the registry kinds;
  - the calls between contracts: `IResults`, `IInstanceEntry`, `IRegistryRead` (with a one-call `bundle`), and `IFate`.
- **Instance slots are reused, measured on the local node.** A key zeroed and then written again costs **exactly as a new key**. A key that kept its value costs an overwrite. So nothing is ever zeroed, and every record keeps a non-zero bit. The generation lives in the instance id and the header, never in a key. §2.1 of the document gives the gating table that makes an old instance unreachable.
- **`docs/architecture/ENG-01-interfaces.md`** contains:
  - the boundaries and access control, and the class sizes;
  - every layout;
  - the entrypoints, events, snapshot and results;
  - randomness and accounts, and M-1…M-6;
  - the slot table for every entrypoint and the budget for every entrypoint;
  - the 40M batch bound checked in slots and in gas;
  - 14 escalations, and the answers to design/02's list.
- **35 tests with budgets, all passing.** They cover:
  - storage addresses (names and keys) of every contract;
  - the size of each record in slots;
  - the bit offsets of every packed record;
  - the keys and data of all 13 events;
  - the batch codec;
  - the provider's refusals;
  - two probes that price one call between contracts.

## Deviations from the brief

- **No stop at review point (a).** Every open choice had a default that the code follows, so I pushed and kept going. The escalations list those defaults.
- **`grimworld_logic` now depends on `starknet` and holds the calls between contracts.** This keeps ADR-0007's rule that neither domain package depends on the other.
- **The measurement probes live in the ephemeral package** (`probes.cairo`). The alternative was a new package, which would mean editing the workspace manifest. `contracts/tools/` holds the three scripts.
- **The provider `TxHashFate` is implemented, not a stub.** It is three lines, and its mainnet refusal is the security check ADR-0002 asks for.
- **Registry field layouts** are given as fields within fixed part counts (§3.5). The bit layout inside each part is ENG-03's.
- **design/02's "each action emits what it emits alone"** is answered with `BatchPlayed` only: no per-kill or per-reveal events (§5).

## Escalations

Details and costs are in §11 of the document.

- **E-1:** a goblin's first record in a slot is a new slot. A batch can create 16 of them (+6.74M) that no weight counts. I recommend weighing them.
- **E-2:** the roster of displaced goblins is bounded at 30. What happens to a 31st is a game rule to decide.
- **E-3:** at most 2 packs of up to 5 goblins, and at most 3 objects, per chunk. This constrains ENG-05.
- **E-4:** the instance clock is capped at 2^28 − 1 ticks.
- **E-5:** the ephemeral domain reads the registry during play, with one `bundle` call per invocation. If content is updated mid-instance, outcomes can change (determinism).
- **E-6:** `barter` calls the hub during play, because the collector's price is in the pack.
- **E-7:** `enter` goes over its budget: 3.38M on a reused slot against 1.9M.
- **E-8:** Fate actions go over their budget: 3.34M against 2.9M.
- **E-9:** version 1's attempt-stable Fate is not met by a synchronous `fate(domain)`. This is ADR-0002's to decide.
- **E-10 (shared file):** `.github/workflows/ci.yml` needs one step, `python3 contracts/tools/class_sizes.py`, after the build.
- **E-11:** market lots are new slots, and a trade side is bounded at 7 entities and 2 balances.
- **E-12:** a reveal weighs 2 in the bound, but costs about 1.4M the first time and 0.6M after.
- **E-13:** design/02's tests of batch equivalence, the gas proof and the region tests need game logic (ENG-05/06/07).
- **E-14:** quiver's components are not embedded (that is ARC's work).

## Open questions

- **CB-2 and CB-3 stay open.** Whether a tick inside a batch shares its reads and writes decides whether 40M holds. They need `play`'s full tick measured in a batch.
- **The reuse measurement comes from the local node's meter.** Its per-slot figures agree with FND-04's Sepolia fit, but the same probe has not been run on Sepolia.
- **Tick and generation rules may move to library classes.** If the full `Instances` would pass the class limit, those rules go to library classes called with `library_call`. Their call cost is assumed to be about one contract call (E); it is not measured.

### Each fix, and where it is

- **F-1 (major), belt settlement**
  - The rule, as the ruling states it:
    - the belt is reserved at entry: `Hub.enter` debits the pack in the entry transaction;
    - the closing report credits the unused counts back;
    - consumed potions are gone;
    - a location transition carries the reserve into the next instance;
    - two slots holding the same item are one debit and one credit of their sum.
  - Storage cost: at most 4 pack pages and `pack_lanes`, all overwrites (+0.16M on `enter` and on `leave`).
  - Code:
    - `Results.belt: [u8; 4]` in `contracts/logic/src/interface.cairo`;
    - the count to carry per slot is lane 4 of the adventurer's `belt` word;
    - the snapshot's docs are updated.
  - Document: §6, and the `enter` and `leave` rows of §9.3 and §10.
  - **Defeat is stopped, as the ruling asks.** design/03 and design/07 do not settle what happens to the belt on defeat. It is escalated as **E-15**, with options (credit back, lose, lose only in a sealed Red Rift) and their cost. The interface carries the counts whichever rule is chosen.
- **F-2 (major), per-batch union**
  - §9.1 separates per-tick, per-batch and lifetime-initialisation bounds.
  - §9.2 per tick: 8 acting goblins + 6 hit by the action → 28 goblin words, 4 `features` words, 4 member words.
  - §9.2 per batch (at most 4 reveals, a union of at most 9 chunks):
    - without a cap, 140 goblins, 310 words: 8.98M when every key already exists, 127M when none does;
    - with the proposed cap of 16 goblins, 60 words.
  - §9.2 lifetime: 4,971 keys at most for one slot (2.25G L2 gas, $1.99). Region 1 uses about 1,099 of them ($0.44).
  - A code change bounds the union: a pack's shared `alert` (3 bits in `PackPlacement`) means alerting a pack writes one `features` word, not one record per goblin.
  - Recomputed: §10.1 (the 40M bound: 40.28M to 46.83M with each tick as measured alone, 21.69M to 28.24M with ticks shared), **E-1**, the new **E-16** (a cap of 16 goblins per batch, recommended), and §10.2 (S1 ≈ 630.7M ≈ $0.556; an adventurer's first expedition +58.6M once).
- **F-3 (major), the standalone actions by their real behaviour**
  - `loot`: 0 world ticks, a draw.
  - `open`: 1 tick (*Interact*), then a draw.
  - `mine`: 3 ticks, interrupted by a hit, no draw.
  - `barter`: 1 tick, then the hub exchange.
  - Where: the table in §4.1; their rows in §9.3 (the world's writes, the results writes, new records) and §10, with and without goblins nearby.
  - **E-8 corrected.** New **E-18**: `mine` draws nothing but design/02 sends it alone as a Fate action.
- **F-4 (major), complete write sets, cold and initialised**
  - §9.3 gives every row both cases. Now covered:
    - `stow` into an empty vault: 11 new slots;
    - `ItemBase.owner` on every equipment transfer;
    - the allocation counters of `register`, `create_adventurer` and a first entry;
    - a first market posting's seller page, escrow page and counters;
    - `identify` setting the `IDENTIFIED` flag.
  - Task pages are written only as needed, never initialised ahead: a page first used later is new then.
  - The roster needs no reset at entry. It is a compact list gated by `header.roster_count`, so `create` writes 17 + ⌈t/4⌉ keys; the first draft counted 21.
  - Code: every counter is now a `Counter` record carrying the non-zero `LIVE` bit, so a count that falls to 0 and rises again is never charged as a new slot (`open_lot_count` was exposed). Covered: `next_*`, `lot_count`, `open_lot_count`, `trade_count` and the registry's `last_ids`. The market writes its three counters at deployment.
  - Dependent budgets regenerated: `enter` 3.48M, or 11.52M the first time; `leave` 1.85M; E-7.
- **F-5 (major), every public selector**
  - §9.3 lists every selector of `Instances`, `Hub`, `Market`, `Registry` and `TxHashFate`, with its bounds, reads, writes, cold and initialised key counts, and calls. This includes `set_account_owner`, `delete_adventurer`, `accept_contract`, `claim_title`, `buy_hint`, `decline_trade`, `cancel_trade`, `set_controller`, the admin setters and the market-to-hub calls.

  - `GoblinKilled {#[key] instance_id, entity, caste, tile, by}`, `ChunkRevealed {#[key] instance_id, chunk}` and `Defeated {#[key] instance_id, adventurer_id}`, in `contracts/ephemeral/src/events.cairo` and the `Instances` event enum. Tested by `test_per_action_event_keys_and_data`.

- **F-6 (minor), displaced remains**

- **F-8 (minor), registry allocation**

  - Where: `content::is_sequential`, the `Registry` docs, §3.5.
- **F-9 (minor), bounded deadlines and packers**

  - Boundary tests, one of each kind of field:
    - `test_deadline_boundaries`;
    - refusal tests of a recharge, a goblin deadline, a member deadline, offsets, walls, attributes, pairs, the `LIVE` overflow and a bitmap above bit 249.
  - **E-4 corrected** (§3.1, §11).
- **F-10 (minor), validating encoder**
  - `encode_action` returns `Option` and refuses a direction above 5, a bar slot above 7, a belt slot above 3; `encode_batch` refuses a batch containing one.
  - Tested by `test_encoder_refusals` (`contracts/logic/src/actions.cairo`, §4.1).

### The common cause of F-2, F-3 and F-4: fixed at the root

`contracts/tools/budget_table.py` was rewritten.
- **Keys:** every write is now a physical key with an identity, meaning its storage variable, its map keys and the word of a multi-slot record. Each key carries one rule:
  - `first`: new when cold, overwritten when initialised;
  - `new`: new every time;
  - `old`: exists by construction;
  - `warm`: written only when the slot is initialised, such as a roster page that a swap removal reaches.
- **Branches:** each branch (completion, interruption or refusal, defeat, objective, and their unions) is the union of its parts' key sets, deduplicated by identity. When parts disagree on a key's rule, `new` wins over `old` over `first` over `warm`.
- **Derived counts:** every count comes from those sets. A row's target is its most expensive branch, in each state (cold and initialised).
- **Calldata:** every transaction now includes its serialized arguments at 5,120 L2 gas per felt (FND-04). Examples: `play` 4 felts, `stow` 29, `set_record` 6.
- **Events** are priced from their frozen shapes (keys, selector included, plus data felts) as `1.157 × (23,356 + 5,564 × felts)`. The line fits the two shapes `EventProbe` measured, and the Sepolia ratio is an estimate, so every event price is labelled E.
- **Outputs:** `--keys` prints §9.3's key sets branch by branch (`contracts/tools/budget-keys-output.txt`); without it, the script prints §10's table, §10.1's branches and the checks (`contracts/tools/budget-output.txt`).
