# [Opus 5.5] SPK-2 — Cost spike

## Summary

> **Latest answer (fix loop 3): ADR-0001's threshold does not hold for the native contracts at
> today's prices, and in the worst case no cut to the fight alone makes it hold.**
>
> **Cost.** A 300-action expedition costs **$0.69 to $0.93** natively (1.38× to 1.86× the $0.50).
> The conditions:
> - devnet 0.10.0, through an account compiled with Cairo 2.19;
> - the tick's flood capped at 15 layers (D-127), with every fight at the capped worst case;
> - prices: mainnet L2 gas 21,687,750,610 fri, L1 data gas 1,544,531,203,854 fri, block 15,591,320 at 2026-09-28T19:30Z; STRK $0.04061.
>
> **Equal-allocation reference, not a budget.** $0.50 / 300 = $0.001667, or 1,833,096 L2 gas per action with DA (832 L1 data gas). Measured actions against it:
> - fight at the game's worst tick: 5,156,800 (2.76×);
> - move in a queue of 10 with 8 goblins: 2,012,800 (1.07×);
> - exploring move: 224,800 (0.12×).
>
> With fights at that reference and everything else as measured, S1 still costs $0.635 and S4 $0.631.
>
> **Fight budget per scenario** (the fight L2 gas at which the expedition costs exactly $0.50, everything else and DA as measured):
> - **S1 295,792** (5.7 % of today's fight);
> - **S4 340,592** (6.8 %);
> - S2 2,836,494 and S3 2,862,094 (55 % to 57 %);
> - S5 2,589,914.
>
> **Correlated case.** A cheaper tick also cheapens the queued moves (tick part 1,659,874 L2 gas per tick, from the queue slope). S1 then reaches $0.50 only with the tick's part at **2.4 %** of today's; free ticks leave $0.490. S2 and S3 need the tick's part at about 22 % to 27 %.
>
> Details: the "Fix loop 3" section below, and `docs/research/SPK-2-cost.md` (its summary and §9.4).

The sections below are in the order the work was done.

A throwaway Dojo world in `spikes/SPK-2/` measures what the heaviest ordinary actions cost. It covers:
- the worst-case tick: 15 × 16 window, 8 awake goblins, one shared flood, damage table, conditions;
- queues of 10, 5 and 1 moves, and 10 moves without goblin;
- a brewing step with and without the D-52 signature;
- the hub actions of a day.

Each is measured three ways:
- as snforge tests with budgets (27 tests, all passing, each with `ceil(1.05 × measured)`);
- per call from the snforge trace, in both metering modes;
- as real transactions on a local Katana.

The money uses live mainnet and Sepolia gas prices and the STRK price. Report: `docs/research/SPK-2-cost.md`.

**Verdict: ADR-0001's threshold does not hold at today's prices.** An expedition of 300 actions costs:
- $1.73 to $2.69 with one model per goblin (3.5× to 5.4× the threshold);
- $0.97 to $1.08 with the goblins packed by hand in one model (1.9× to 2.2×).

At 21.5 Gfri per L2 gas and STRK at $0.0408, $0.50 buys 569M L2 gas: 1.9M per action. Storage through the Dojo world is 72 % of the worst-case tick; the whole world tick in memory is 1.25M of 13.2M. R-2 materialises (escalated below).

**D-52: keep it.** It adds 27k to 39k L2 gas per first brew of a pair (in memory, same pair and outcome), +1.6 % at system level: $0.000065.

The session ran as Opus 5.5, as the brief names.

## Deviations from the brief

- **Flood of our own.** `origami_hexmap` 1.8.0 does not build on Cairo 2.13 with our pins, so `src/board.cairo` is a bitboard flood of our own: the library's field-product dilation, rewritten. The brief allows this "stated as such"; it is stated in the code and the report.
- **Window stand-in.** The assembled window is read from one stored model per origin (one read per tick). The brief says the assembly is SPK-7's; the design never stores the window.
- **Extra measurements** that were not asked for, but that decide the verdict:
  - a storage variant: goblins hand-packed in one model;
  - queues of 5 and 1 moves, and 10 moves without goblin;
  - in-memory benchmarks of the flood, the world tick and the discovery.

  These are measurements only; no production code.
- **Queue fixture.** The first formation put a goblin behind the front rank. Under rule (a) it was walled off and fell out of the window. The formation was changed, after a search in memory, so that all 8 goblins stay awake for the 10 ticks, which is the worst case.
- **Budget files.** No `docs/BUDGETS.md` and no per-package `GAS.md` (docs/CAIRO.md §2): `docs/BUDGETS.md` is outside the allowlist, and a throwaway spike has no package to document. The figures are in the research report and in the tests' budget comments.
- **Katana metering.** Katana 1.7.1 meters these transactions in Cairo steps (its figures are snforge's Cairo-steps figures plus a constant 0.27M to 0.32M). So the money gives two figures: Katana's, and a "Sierra estimate" (snforge Sierra gas plus Katana's transaction overhead). Mainnet should lie between the two.

## Escalations

1. **ADR-0001, R-2.** The threshold (300 actions ≤ $0.50, sponsored) is not met on Starknet mainnet at today's prices: 1.9× to 5.4× over, depending on storage and mix. Option B (L3) is to be re-examined, or the threshold and its assumptions restated. This is the owner's call. Two derived figures bound the choice:
   - the break-even L2 gas price at today's STRK price is 5.2 to 11.0 Gfri (it was 17.8 to 30.4 over two weeks);
   - the budget per action at today's prices is 1.9M L2 gas.
2. **`origami_hexmap` 1.8.0 on Cairo 2.13 (D-117, FND-01)**:
   - the scarbs.xyz package depends on `snforge_std` 0.61, which conflicts with `dojo_snf_test` 1.8.0's `snforge_std` 0.51.2;
   - the git tag `v1.8.0` does not compile: `core::internal::bounded_int::BoundedInt` is not visible in `map.cairo`, `helpers/bits.cairo` and `helpers/rng.cairo`.

   The game cannot consume the library with the SPK-5 pins as they stand. FND-01 is to prove the opposite and should hit the same wall. The track LIB orchestrator should know.
3. **PLAN's SPK-2 row** says "10 Rifts"; D-101 says 5 a day per account. 5 was used, as the brief asked. PLAN is a shared file: the row needs correcting.
4. **Rule (a) behaviour** (docs/needs/hexmap.md point 5): the flood on the occupancy frozen at the start of the tick walls a goblin off behind its own pack. Observed: it detours and falls out of the window within 7 ticks. This is a design question, not a cost one, for whoever owns design/04 *Goblin AI*.

## Open questions

- **FND-04.**
  - Write budgets in Sierra gas, with the transaction overhead measured on Sepolia with the real account (Controller session, paymaster)?
  - May hot instance state leave one-model-per-entity? The options are hand-packed felts per instance or per chunk, or raw storage with events for Torii (ADR-0004).
  - The Dojo world costs about 70k plus 26k per field per read, and about 280k plus 35k per field per write call. That is the main cost driver measured.
- **SPK-7.**
  - Measure the window assembly as chunk models read through the world, in this harness (the stand-in costs 95k per tick).
  - Measure the occupied-bit writes on chunk crossings.
  - Settle how awake goblins are found without reading every goblin of the instance.
- **Katana tracks Cairo steps here.** The likely cause is the dev account's Sierra version; this was not checked. SPK-1 or SPK-9 on Sepolia would give the real transaction overhead in Sierra gas.
- **Unexplained overhead.** Katana's transaction overhead is 0.68M to 0.93M for brewing, hub actions and the empty queue, against 0.27M to 0.32M for the heavy ticks.

# Part 2 — the same worst cases as native contracts (ADR-0007, D-123)

## Summary

The same worst cases, measured as native Starknet contracts on Cairo 2.19, with the difference per action against part 1's Dojo baseline.

- **Merge and part 1.** `origin/main` is merged into the branch (no rebase, no conflict). Part 1 stays runnable with its own Dojo pins and copies of `with-katana.sh` and `locked.sh`, and was rerun once to prove it.
- **What part 2 is.** `spikes/SPK-2/native/` holds two contracts:
  - `Hub`, persistent;
  - `Instances`, ephemeral, whose results go back to `Hub` in one call.

  State is packed by hand, access control is our own, and each action emits one small event. The pure logic (flood, tick, damage, discovery, fixtures, tables) is part 1's, copied unchanged and checked byte for byte.
- **How it was measured.** 31 snforge tests with budgets, the per-call trace, and real transactions on starknet-devnet through `scripts/with-node.sh` and `sncast`, with fresh prices.

**Native against Dojo:** every action is cheaper natively. As full transactions (Katana for Dojo, devnet for native), native costs **0.33× to 0.81×** the Dojo figure:
- worst-case tick: 0.70× with a struct per goblin (12.59M against 17.94M), 0.62× packed (4.91M against 7.93M);
- queues with 8 goblins: 0.74× to 0.76×; without goblin: 0.33×;
- brewing: 0.41× to 0.52×; hub actions: 0.40× to 0.81×.

On the contract's own execution (snforge, Sierra gas) the ratio is 0.42× to 0.59× on the tick and 0.13× to 0.20× on hub and brewing actions.

**Native verdict:** the threshold is still not met. At 22,573,370,546 fri per L2 gas and STRK $0.04133903 (mainnet block 15,587,811, 2026-09-28T17:51Z; CoinGecko 17:51:39Z), a 300-action expedition costs **$0.72 to $2.08** natively. That is 1.4× to 4.2× the $0.50, against $1.15 to $2.86 for Dojo at the same prices.

To pass, the L2 gas price would have to fall to 5.4 to 15.7 Gfri (it ranged from 17.9 to 30.5 over two weeks). The cost is no longer storage but the tick's computation (about 1.25M per tick) and the transaction around each action: 1.0M to 2.1M on devnet with packed storage. The 100 fights of an expedition are $0.46 on their own even when packed.

**D-52: unchanged, keep it.** It adds +73,820 at call level and +40,000 on devnet.

## C-6 (major): 1,833,096 is an equal-allocation reference, not the fight budget

**Finding.** 1,833,096 L2 gas per action is $0.50 spread equally over 300 actions. With every other cost and DA fixed, fights at 1.8M still give S1 $0.632 and S4 $0.629 (auditor's figures).

**Fix.** `money_devnet.py` now computes two things:
- **the fight budget per scenario**: the fight L2 gas at which the expedition costs exactly $0.50, everything else and DA as measured;
- **the correlated case**: the tick's part of a transaction is taken as the slope of the checked queues with 8 goblins (1,659,874 L2 gas per tick, least squares on 1, 5 and 10 moves). It is scaled by one factor r in fights, in queued moves near goblins and in the other tick-bearing transactions; the rest stays as measured. The model is linear over receipts, which is stated as an assumption.

1,833,096 is renamed the **equal-allocation reference** everywhere it is used. The fight-only conclusion ("it would hold with fights near 1.8M") is withdrawn in `docs/research/SPK-2-cost.md` (summary, §9.3, new §9.4) and in this report (struck through where it appeared).

**Evidence** (`money-devnet-output.txt`):

## C-1 (minor): invocation figures are not additive

**Finding.** A transaction's fee is a weighted maximum over resources (<https://docs.starknet.io/learn/protocol/fees>), so the trace's invocation figures do not sum to the receipt. The capped serpentine trace leaves −592,000.

**Fix.**
- In `docs/research/SPK-2-cost.md` §8.2, the difference is now labelled the **non-additive residual** (receipt minus the sum of the invocation figures), explicitly not the cost of anything.
- Withdrawn: the "invocation gas in steps of 40,000" rounding explanation (§8.2, §9.2), the "account and protocol 0.76M to 0.88M" allocation, and the reading of the residual as calldata, events and state diff. None was derived from resource vectors and the fee formula.
- The same labels and caveat now appear in `money_devnet.py`'s trace table and in `devnet_measure.py`'s docstring (the JSON field keeps its name, `rest`, documented as the residual).
- Stated: every cost, ratio and scenario uses receipts only.
