# Credits

## Art

- **Tiny Swords** by **Pixel Frog** — <https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords>.
  The prototype uses the pack's units and creatures, and sprites derived from them, under the
  pack's licence: free to use in personal and commercial projects and to modify. The pack is
  **not redistributed**: neither it nor anything derived from it is stored in this repository.
  It lives in a private repository attached as the `assets` submodule, and the game ships
  sprites packed into atlases inside its app bundle (`tools/art/README.md`).

## Fonts

- **Pixelify Sans** by **The Pixelify Sans Project Authors** (<https://github.com/eifetx/Pixelify-Sans>),
  under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, from the npm package `@fontsource/pixelify-sans`. The client
  bundles the latin subset (weights 600 and 700, `woff2`) for titles, ribbons, buttons and the HUD's
  figures, and serves it from the site itself.
