## The expedition: 300 actions (SPK-1 §5's composition), burner sending directly

S1: 36 queues of 5 moves near goblins, 100 fights, 20 other actions (a move alone near goblins), enter, leave.
S2: 12 queues of 5 near goblins, 12 exploring queues of 10, the rest as S1.

Every batched row assumes a batch keeps **one** tick's state remainder and one tick's DA footprint (832 L1 data gas): the tick's 12 slots changed once per transaction. ## What $0.50 leaves, at these prices

- The target: 567,809,541 L2 gas per expedition with no DA (D).
- S1, fights batched and shared (E): 654,702,488 L2 gas and 54,976 L1 data gas, $0.579; over the target by 89,417,521 L2 gas.
- S2, fights batched and shared (E): 445,374,692 L2 gas and 38,848 L1 data gas, $0.394; under the target by 120,650,895 L2 gas.
- S1's 36 queues of 5 near goblins cost 358,026,084 (D): 43.554 % of S1 with fights batched, each tick as measured, with the 27 felts (E), and 54.685 % with fights batched and shared (E).
- For S1 to meet $0.50 with its moves near goblins in tens (E): a batch of 10 fight ticks at 16,027,465 L2 gas, **1,469,435 per tick** (game call, inside the batch), against 3,564,913 measured alone and 1,705,764 if shared as the queue shares.
- Break-even L2 gas price, S1 and S2 with fights batched and shared (E): 18.4 and 27.1 Gfri (today 21.3).
